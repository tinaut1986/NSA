- Lists open at the top while they load and sort; floating arrows (and ZL / ZR on a New 3DS) jump to the start or the end.
- Your own themes: Settings -> Edit the theme changes every colour with a finger and shows it at once on both screens, from any theme or a blank template; the web page has the same editor. They are kept as files in /nsa/themes/.
- HOME theme: icon grids in pages that turn sideways (tap a dot, or drag the bar, to jump to one), and sheets close when pulled down.
- Pixel theme: the digits, B and Z are drawn by hand, so 5 and S, 8 and B no longer look alike.
