- NSA speaks 11 languages, chosen from a list in Settings: English, Spanish, Catalan, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese and Chinese (simplified and traditional); "System" follows the console's own.
- The web page gains a System tab (console, network, storage, battery), plays the console's music, groups updates and DLC under their game, remembers the titles' names and icons, and shows transfers as a toast.
- Installing from a URL or QR asks how (Install, Save and install, Save, install and delete, or Save); Dropbox and Google Drive links work.
- Faster downloads, FTP and sends to another 3DS, one transfer at a time and a steady speed; whole folders can be sent. Sending to another 3DS needs this version on both consoles.
- Back arrows in Settings, Remote install and the changelog; the music no longer pauses for FTP unless you ask (an Old 3DS is asked once).
