- Send several titles to another 3DS at once: mark them in Titles (the tick icon at the top) and press START. The other console accepts them all with one answer and picks for each one, from its free space, whether it installs as it arrives or as a package.
- Titles: holding A with titles marked offers what can be done to all of them: send them, export them as CIAs or delete them.
- Download queue: downloads go on in the background, one after another, from the Store or Install from URL. Pause, resume or reorder them from its icon in the main menu or from the web page.
- Store: an app's pictures, and Uninstall the CIA / Delete the 3DSX for what the Store installed.
- Archives: View contents shows what a zip holds, and Install from URL and Remote install extract it into place on the SD card (an app into /3ds/<app>/).
- Every screen's buttons stay readable at the bottom, and the (i) at its left lists them all.
