- System is now three groups: Information (console, network, storage, battery), Console settings (Date and time, Wi-Fi connections) and Hardware tests.
- Buttons and touch: every key, the Circle Pad, the C-Stick, the touch screen and the 3D and volume sliders, live, with a counter of what is left to try and a magnified view of each stick's centre to spot drift.
- Motion sensors: the accelerometer and the gyroscope live, and a recording of both to a CSV file in /nsa/logs that keeps going with the lid closed.
- Guide: a chapter for the hardware tests, and the Wi-Fi and Date and time tours go through Console settings.
